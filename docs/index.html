About Us

Ikorodu’s digital property marketplace

Ikorodu Realtors is an independent digital property marketing and listing platform focused on connecting prospective property buyers, tenants and investors with property opportunities across Ikorodu, Lagos State, Nigeria.

The platform provides a centralised online space where users can explore available houses, apartments, land and commercial properties through property descriptions, asking prices, photographs, videos and relevant location information.
