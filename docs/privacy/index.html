Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Ikorodu Realtors respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it and the choices you have, in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

Information we collect

We collect the information you choose to send us when you enquire about a property or ask to list one, such as your name, phone number, email address, the property you are interested in and the contents of your message. This may be sent through WhatsApp, email or the forms on this website.

We also receive property information, photographs and videos from listing partners for publication.

Like most websites, our hosting and third-party services (such as map tiles and photographs loaded from other providers) may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser type.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiries and arrange property inspections.
  • To connect you with the representative responsible for a property, where needed to complete your enquiry.
  • To receive, review and manage property listings submitted by partners.
  • To improve our website and meet legal obligations.

Sharing

We share your details with the listing representative responsible for a property only where needed to respond to your enquiry or arrange an inspection. We do not sell your personal information. We may use service providers for communication and website hosting, and messages sent through WhatsApp are also subject to WhatsApp's own privacy practices.

Retention and security

We keep enquiry information only for as long as needed for the purposes above or as the law requires, and take reasonable steps to protect it from loss or misuse.

Your rights

You may ask to access, correct or delete your personal information, to object to certain uses, or to withdraw consent, by contacting us. You may also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

Children

This website is not directed at children under 18 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy from time to time. Questions or requests can be sent to ikorodurealtors@gmail.com or WhatsApp +234 913 925 5152.
